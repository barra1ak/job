Step 1

Bring a real job.

Don't practice with a pretend posting. Find a position you could genuinely imagine applying for and paste the description below.

When possible, go directly to the employer's career page and work from the original posting.
Step 2

Make AI your job-description decoder.

Use AI to figure out what the employer is really emphasizing so you know what deserves attention in your application.

Build your prompt

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT or the AI tool you use. Your job description will automatically be included. 

Act as a resume and job-description analyst.

Review the job description below and identify:

1. The 10-15 most important skills, qualifications, tools, and recurring phrases.
2. Which keywords appear most important based on repetition and placement.
3. Which requirements are essential versus preferred.
4. Where these concepts could naturally appear in a resume IF they truthfully reflect my experience.
5. Important language I should understand before writing a customized cover letter.

Do not invent experience or credentials for me.
Do not keyword-stuff.
I want the application to remain accurate and human.

JOB DESCRIPTION:

Capture your keywords

When AI gives you the results, add the most important skills and phrases here.

⚠️ Don't turn this into keyword bingo.

The goal isn't to trick an applicant tracking system. It's to understand the employer's language and use it naturally when it accurately describes your experience.

Step 4

Now find the humans.

Use LinkedIn as more than a job board. Use it to identify the actual people connected to the organization and the hiring process.

🏢

Follow the company

Follow the organization's page so hiring updates, company news, and employee posts begin appearing in your feed.

🎓

Find common ground

Search for alumni, mutual connections, shared organizations, or people with similar backgrounds.

🧲

Find recruiting people

Look for talent acquisition, university recruiting, early-career recruiting, or an appropriate HR contact.
